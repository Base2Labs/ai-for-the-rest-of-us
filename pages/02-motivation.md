# What changed

---

# Two camps, both wrong

One side says it's slop, the other says it replaces everyone — and the honest answer moves every few months.

---

# Six months, zero lines of code by hand

Where this comes from: an experiment, not a survey — and the deck you're looking at was itself assembled by an agent reading my notes. More on that at the end.

---

# Four quadrants of skill

Syntax and algorithms got cheap; problem decomposition and systems thinking didn't — and value flows to whatever stayed scarce.

---

# The bottleneck moved — it didn't disappear

Say the fear out loud first — *is this coming for my job too?* — then answer it: the work went upstream to deciding what to build and downstream to keeping it alive.

---

# This already left software

Paramedics, glaciologists and restaurant operators are building their own tools now — and the measured effect on their days wasn't emptiness, it was intensity.
