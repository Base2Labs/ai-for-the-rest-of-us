# What We'll Cover

## Presentation Points

1. Understanding the Landscape — What AI is good at (and isn't)
2. Determinism — Making AI output predictable
3. Context Management — Feeding AI the right information
4. Agents — Beyond autocomplete
5. Metacognition — AI as a thinking partner
6. Tools & Demos — What's available today
7. Productivity & Process — How workflows change
8. What's Next — Where this is all heading

## Presenter Notes

Here's the roadmap for today. We'll start by understanding which tasks AI handles well and which it struggles with — that framing will make everything else click. Then we'll get into the practical mechanics: how to get more deterministic output, how to manage context effectively, and what agents actually are. We'll talk about metacognition — using AI not just to write code but to deepen your own understanding. I'll demo some tools, and we'll close with how this changes our day-to-day processes and where things are heading.
