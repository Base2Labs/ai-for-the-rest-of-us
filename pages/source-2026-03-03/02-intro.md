# The Experiment

## Presentation Points

- 6 months coding exclusively with AI agents
- Zero lines of code written by hand
- Goal: separate hype from reality through hands-on experience

## Presenter Notes

For the past six months I've been running an experiment — I committed to coding exclusively through AI agents. I did not directly touch any code. My goal was to develop real, practical knowledge of these tools by going all in, not just dabbling. I wanted to find out for myself what works, what doesn't, and where the real value is. The best way to cut through the noise is to build something real with these tools and see what happens.
