# The Two Camps

## Presentation Points

- Camp 1: "AI Slop" — AI output is unreliable, low quality, overhyped
- Camp 2: "AI Second Coming" — AI will replace developers entirely
- The truth is somewhere in between
- The truth is also a moving target

## Presenter Notes

You've probably noticed two very vocal camps in this debate. On one side, people dismiss AI-generated code as slop — unreliable, messy, not production-worthy. On the other side, people act like AI is about to replace every developer. The reality is somewhere in between, and crucially, it's shifting fast. What was true six months ago isn't necessarily true today. This material is sprawling and will cover a lot of ground — you'll probably leave with more questions than answers, and that's okay. The goal is to give you a foundation to start experimenting on your own.
