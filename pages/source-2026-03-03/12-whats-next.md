# What's Next

## Presentation Points

- **OpenClaw** — Understanding the agentic development loop
- **Figma MCP** — Generating accurate UIs from design files
- **Local LLMs** — Running models locally for speed, privacy, and cost
- **From AI-assisted to fully agentic** — The trajectory we're on
- The goal: move from augmentation to safe, autonomous delegation

## Presenter Notes

Looking ahead, there are a few things I'm exploring and excited about. OpenClaw is about understanding the full agentic development loop — how agents plan, execute, and iterate. Understanding this loop is key to getting better at steering it.

Figma MCP is a practical one — using Figma's Model Context Protocol integration to generate UIs directly from design work. This closes the gap between what's designed and what's built, which is a persistent source of back-and-forth in most teams.

Local LLMs are becoming viable for certain tasks. Running models locally gives you speed, privacy, and cost benefits, especially for repetitive or sensitive work that doesn't need the largest frontier models.

The broader trajectory is clear: we're moving from AI-assisted flows — where humans drive and AI suggests — to fully agentic flows where AI drives and humans review. The challenge is getting there safely. We need the patterns, the guardrails, and the confidence in output quality before we can delegate more. That's the work ahead for all of us.
