# What a model actually is

---

# Text in, text out

That is the entire interface — every product you've used is built on top of that one function.

---

# It remembers nothing

There is no session inside the model; the whole conversation is re-sent from scratch on every single turn.

---

# It predicts — it doesn't look things up

Which is exactly why it can be fluent and wrong in the same sentence, and why the next act is about giving it hands.
